FULL DESCRIPTION  (4000 chars max)
────────────────────────────────────
Connected Steps is Hyderabad's community running app — built for serious runners who want to train together, stay accountable, and push each other further.

Whether you're chasing your first 5K or training for a full marathon, Connected Steps gives you everything you need in one place.

━━━ WHAT YOU GET ━━━

🏃 Group Training Sessions
Join scheduled morning runs 3× a week. RSVP in the app, scan a QR code to mark attendance, and earn points every session you show up to.

🏆 Live Leaderboard
See how you rank against the community — by week, month, or all-time. Compete with friends or follow the top runners for motivation.

💬 Direct Coach Chat
Message your assigned running coach directly inside the app. Ask about your training plan, your next race, or anything running-related.

📊 Progress Tracking
Your attendance history, total sessions, total points, and personal bests — all in one place. Link your Strava account to sync runs automatically.

🎯 Milestone Badges
Earn badges for your 1st session, 5th, 10th, 25th, and 50th. See your community's achievements in the activity feed.

📅 Events
Register for upcoming races and community events without leaving the app.

🤝 Community Feed
Stay connected between sessions — see who attended, who uploaded photos, and who just crossed a milestone.

👥 Referral Rewards
Invite friends and earn bonus points for every person who joins and attends their first session.

━━━ HOW IT WORKS ━━━

1. Sign up with your email
2. Get assigned to a coach
3. Join sessions and scan in with your phone's camera
4. Watch your points and rank grow
5. Stay connected with your coach and community

━━━ FOR WHOM ━━━

Connected Steps is for runners in Hyderabad who want more than just a solo training app. It's for people who believe running is better together — and that showing up for your community makes you a better athlete.

Sessions currently run at Kondapur and surrounding areas of Hyderabad.

━━━ MEMBERSHIP ━━━

A Connected Steps membership gives you access to all group training sessions, coach support, and community features. Monthly, quarterly, and annual plans are available.

━━━ PRIVACY ━━━

We collect only what we need: your name, email, phone number, and training data. We never sell your data. Full privacy policy at connectedsteps.in/privacy.

━━━ CONTACT ━━━

Questions? Email us at connected.steps2106@gmail.com or message your coach directly in the app.

Run further. Together.
