# Connected Steps — Volunteer Manual

**Version 3.0 · 2026-07-28**

---

## Introduction

As a Connected Steps volunteer, you help run event day operations — BIB collection, t-shirt distribution, participant check-in, and breakfast verification. This manual covers your role and the tools available to you.

---

## Getting Started

### Mobile App

Download the Connected Steps volunteer app on your phone. The admin team will provide login credentials.

On race day:
1. Open the app
2. Log in with your volunteer credentials
3. Your assigned station will appear on the home screen

### Web Portal (Alternative)

If you are using a tablet or shared device, open a browser and go to the volunteer portal URL provided by your event coordinator.

---

## Check-In Station

Your main job at the check-in station is to verify participant identity and mark them as arrived.

### QR Code Scan

1. Ask the participant to show their registration QR code (from the Connected Steps app or confirmation email)
2. Point your camera at the QR code
3. The app shows the participant's name and registration details
4. Confirm the details match the participant
5. Tap **Check In**
6. The participant's status changes to "Checked In" in real time

### Manual Check-In (QR not available)

If a participant cannot show their QR code:
1. Tap **Manual Search** on the check-in screen
2. Search by full name, email, or registration code
3. Confirm identity with a photo ID
4. Tap **Check In**

### What to do if check-in fails

- "Already checked in" — participant may have checked in at another gate; confirm with the coordinator
- "Registration not found" — search again with a different spelling; if still not found, escalate to coordinator
- "Waitlisted" — participant is not confirmed; direct to the coordinator desk

---

## BIB Collection Station

Participants collect their race BIB (number bib) before the event.

1. Ask for the participant's name or registration code
2. Find them in the BIB assignment list (search in the app)
3. Hand them the pre-assigned BIB
4. Tap **Mark BIB Collected** to update their record

If a participant requests a BIB swap, direct them to the coordinator — only admins can change BIB assignments.

---

## T-Shirt Collection Station

1. Scan or search for the participant
2. The app shows their confirmed t-shirt size
3. Hand them the correct size
4. Tap **Mark T-Shirt Collected**

**Important:** Only hand out the size shown in the app. If a participant wants to change size, they must speak to the coordinator.

If a t-shirt size is out of stock:
1. Tap **Report Out of Stock**
2. The coordinator will be notified automatically
3. Offer the nearest available size — **only with the participant's agreement**

---

## Breakfast / Refreshment Verification

At the breakfast station, each participant is entitled to one meal.

1. Scan the participant's QR code
2. The app shows if they have already collected breakfast
3. If not collected: tap **Mark Collected**
4. If already collected: show "Already collected" message — do not give another serving

---

## Results Collection

After the race, participants may visit the results station.

1. Search for the participant
2. The app shows their finish time and category rank
3. Direct them to the certificate download link in the app
4. If their result is missing, direct them to the coordinator

---

## Offline Mode

The Connected Steps volunteer app works offline. If your internet connection is lost:
- Scans are saved locally
- When connection is restored, they sync automatically
- The sync indicator at the top of the screen shows "Synced" when up to date

**Important:** Do not uninstall or force-close the app while there are unsynced scans.

---

## Escalation Points

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Participant disputes their registration | Coordinator desk |
| Duplicate BIB found | Halt both participants, call coordinator |
| App crashes or stops responding | Restart app; use manual paper list as backup |
| Medical emergency | Call emergency services first; then radio coordinator |
| Aggressive or difficult participant | Call coordinator immediately; do not argue |

---

## End of Shift

1. Tap **End Shift** in the app
2. Confirm your final sync is complete (sync indicator shows "Synced")
3. Return any physical materials to the coordinator
4. Complete your volunteer sign-off with the coordinator

Thank you for your service — you're what makes race day work!
