# Connected Steps — Participant Manual

**Version 3.0 · 2026-07-28**

---

## Welcome

Connected Steps is the platform powering your running events. This guide helps you register for events, track your progress, and get ready for race day.

---

## Getting Started

### Download the App

The Connected Steps mobile app is available on:
- **Android**: Google Play Store — search "Connected Steps"
- **iOS**: Apple App Store — search "Connected Steps"

Or use the web app at `https://www.connectedsteps.in` from your mobile browser.

### Create Your Account

1. Open the app
2. Tap **Sign Up**
3. Enter your email and set a password
4. Verify your email (check your inbox for a link)
5. Complete your profile: name, phone number, date of birth, gender

Your profile information is used on your race certificate and BIB. Make sure it's accurate.

---

## Registering for an Event

1. Open the app and tap **Events** or browse to the event page
2. Find the event you want to join
3. Tap **Register**
4. Choose your race category (5K, 10K, Half Marathon, etc.)
5. Select your t-shirt size (if applicable)
6. Fill in any additional required information
7. For paid events: complete payment via Razorpay (cards, UPI, wallets all accepted)
8. You receive a confirmation email with your registration code and QR code

**Tip:** Save the confirmation email — your QR code is your entry pass for race day.

---

## Your Registration QR Code

Your QR code is unique to your registration. You need it to:
- Check in at the event gate
- Collect your BIB
- Collect your t-shirt
- Collect breakfast/refreshments (where applicable)

Find it in:
- The confirmation email (PDF attachment or inline)
- The Connected Steps app → My Events → [Event Name] → Show QR Code

If you lose the email: log in to the app and access your QR code at any time.

## Race Day

### What to Bring

- Your QR code (screenshot on phone or printout)
- Photo ID (for identity verification if needed)
- Any kit you ordered (t-shirt sizes shown in app)

### Check-In Process

1. Arrive at the event venue at the time specified in your confirmation email
2. Join the check-in queue
3. Show your QR code to the volunteer scanner
4. Wait for the "Checked In" confirmation
5. Collect your BIB from the BIB collection station
6. Collect your t-shirt (if ordered) from the t-shirt station

### After the Race

Results are typically published within 2–6 hours. You will receive a notification when your results are available.

Find your results:
- App → My Events → [Event Name] → Results
- You'll see your finish time, pace, and category rank

---

## Certificates

Certificates are generated after results are published. To download yours:
1. App → My Events → [Event Name] → Certificate
2. Tap **Download PDF**

Certificates include your name, finish time, event details, and a unique verification code. Make sure your profile name is correctly set before race day — the name on your profile is the name on your certificate.

## Tracking Your Progress

### Leaderboard

The leaderboard shows the top runners in your community. Points are earned for:
- Completing events
- Training sessions attended
- Referrals
- Challenges completed

Find the leaderboard: App → Community → Leaderboard

### Activity Tracking

Connect your fitness device to automatically sync your training:
- **Strava** — App → Profile → Connected Apps → Connect Strava
- **Garmin** — same path
- **Apple Health** — App → Profile → Connected Apps → Apple Health (iOS only)
- **Google Health Connect** — App → Profile → Connected Apps → Health Connect (Android only)
- **Fitbit** — App → Profile → Connected Apps → Connect Fitbit

Synced activities appear in your activity feed and contribute to your points total.

## Membership

A Connected Steps membership gives you:
- Priority registration for popular events
- Discounted event fees
- Access to premium training content
- Monthly points bonus

To subscribe: App → Profile → Membership → View Plans

## Referrals

Earn bonus points by referring friends:
1. App → Profile → My Referral Code
2. Share your code
3. When a friend signs up and registers for their first event, you both earn bonus points

---

## Communication Preferences

To manage which notifications and emails you receive:
1. App → Profile → Notification Settings
2. Toggle on/off: event reminders, session reminders, community activity, weekly digest

You can also unsubscribe from emails using the link at the bottom of any email we send you.

## Getting Help

### In-App Support

App → Help → Submit a Question

### Common Issues

**"My QR code is not scanning"**  
Increase screen brightness and make sure the QR code is fully visible in the camera frame. If it still doesn't work, ask the volunteer to search for you by name or email.

**"My payment was deducted but I didn't get a confirmation"**  
Wait 10 minutes — payment confirmation is automatic but may be delayed. If you still don't receive it, contact us via in-app support with your payment reference number.

**"I want to change my t-shirt size"**  
Size changes are possible up to 3 days before the event. Contact support via the app.

**"I want to cancel my registration"**  
Contact us via in-app support. Refunds follow the event's refund policy (shown on the event page at registration time).

**"My results are not showing up"**  
Results may take up to 6 hours after the event to be published. If they're still missing after 24 hours, contact support with your registration code.
